Project Eidolon: Requirements Baseline

1. Project Objective

Project Eidolon is a computational aircraft-design study focused on a two-seat light aircraft.

The objective is to develop a defensible preliminary design that satisfies the assigned mission requirements while investigating opportunities to improve aerodynamic efficiency, propulsion matching, structural mass and overall aircraft performance.

The HANSA-NG will serve as a comparison aircraft. Any claims of superior performance must be supported by comparable operating conditions and documented calculations.

3. Initial Design Configuration

The initial configuration is a two-seat, single-engine, propeller-driven aircraft.

The following are preliminary design choices, not finalized selections:

* Wing airfoil: NACA 23012 baseline, with NACA 63-415 under investigation.
* Wing area: approximately 14.15 m².
* Aspect ratio: approximately 8.55.
* Engine: candidate selection pending.
* Propeller: candidate selection pending.
* Structural materials: selection pending.

4. Engineering Evidence Requirements

Every major design decision must identify:

1. The requirement being addressed.
2. The assumptions used.
3. The calculation or analysis method.
4. The resulting numerical evidence.
5. The limitations and uncertainties.
6. The decision made from the evidence.

Calculated outputs must not be described as experimentally validated unless appropriate validation has been performed.

5. Design Comparison

Eidolon will be compared with the HANSA-NG using documented specifications and consistent operating conditions.

Comparison metrics may include:

* Maximum takeoff weight.
* Empty weight.
* Cruise speed.
* Maximum speed.
* Range.
* Rate of climb.
* Stall speed.
* Fuel consumption.
* Payload.
* Aerodynamic efficiency.

Unavailable or unverified competitor specifications must be identified explicitly.

6. Current Project Status

The repository contains preliminary Python sizing and propulsion scripts, an airfoil coordinate library and a constraint-analysis graph.

The aerodynamic model, propulsion model, weight estimates and integrated aircraft-performance predictions require further verification.

The current design is preliminary and is not a finalized or validated aircraft configuration.
